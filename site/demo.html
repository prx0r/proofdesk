ProofDesk Live

Evidence-gated document automation. Nutrient DWS extracts grounded evidence. ProofDesk decides when that evidence is sufficient to act.

THE PROBLEM

Extraction accuracy is not execution authority

An AI agent receives a procurement instruction. It can extract data, generate agreements, merge documents. But signing is irreversible — it creates a legal commitment.

ProofDesk inserts a server-side authority gate between reversible document work and irreversible action. The agent can prepare everything. It cannot act alone.

"Prepare the Northstar vendor agreement for $42,500 and send it to our CFO."
STAGE 1

Nutrient DWS extracts grounded evidence

Every extracted value retains its source page, bounding box, and extraction confidence. Not just text — evidence with provenance.

STAGE 2

Cross-document contradiction detected

Six deterministic assertions check the extracted facts. Five pass. One fails — not because of extraction error, but because the documents conflict with each other.

STAGE 3

Authority gate blocks the action

Six conditions enforced server-side. The agent cannot negotiate. High confidence does not become execution authority.

ACTION BLOCKED

STAGE 4

Human reviews the exact evidence

Not the entire document bundle. The specific failing claim, its source, and the confidence scores.

ACTION AUTHORIZED

STAGE 5

Every transition is auditable

Hash-chained events. Merkle proofs. Content-addressed artifacts. Change one byte and the hash changes.
