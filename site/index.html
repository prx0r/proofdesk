what's under the hood
proofdesk isn't just if/else rules. behind the authority gate is a research program in calibrated confidence — when can an automated system be trusted to act, and when must it abstain?
confidence algorithms
conformal risk controlangelopoulos et al., iclr 2024 — finite-sample risk guarantees
dual-call confidenceextractconf style — hunter + mapper extraction verification
per-field risk budgetssigner: 1%, amount: 2%, date: 3%, default: 5%
isotonic calibrationscore mapping from raw confidence to true correctness probability
sheepish transformasymmetric overconfidence penalty — our contribution
online calibrationmarginonlinecalibrator — learns from human feedback
how the sheepish metric works
standard calibration treats overconfidence and underconfidence symmetrically. but in document automation, overconfident errors are operationally more dangerous than cautious abstentions. the sheepish transform applies an asymmetric penalty: confidence that is too high gets penalized more than confidence that is too low.
raw_confidence: 0.85
field_accuracy: 0.72
match_score: 0.90
grounding: 0.80

standard calibration: 0.85 → 0.82
sheepish calibration: 0.85 → 0.74 (penalizes overconfidence)
per-field risk budgets
different fields have different risk profiles. a wrong signer name is catastrophic. a wrong metadata field is tolerable. proofdesk applies per-field thresholds based on operational impact.
signer1% max error rate — signing is irreversible
amount2% — financial impact
date3% — temporal fields
party_name2% — entity identification
document_body5% — general content
metadata10% — low-impact fields
convergence loop
human decisions become calibration data. each time a person resolves an exception, proofdesk receives a labeled observation: "at this confidence level, was trusting this evidence actually justified?" the online calibrator updates, and future decisions improve.

the evidence-to-authority gap
this is the core research question: when is a correctly extracted document fact sufficient evidence for an automated action? extraction accuracy ≠ action safety. proofdesk measures the gap between "we extracted it correctly" and "we should act on it."
