## Challenge Description (word-for-word)

Every business relies on software—but much of it is outdated, slow, expensive, or frustrating to use.

Your challenge is simple:

Pick a business application you use today and build a better version with AI.

Reimagine how AI can transform the software people use every day. Whether you're replacing a CRM, support desk, HR system, procurement portal, project management tool, or something entirely different, show us what business software should look like in the AI era.

Need inspiration? (Ideas—not requirements)

- A CRM that summarizes customer interactions, suggests next steps, and automates follow-up.
- A help desk that routes tickets, drafts responses, and surfaces relevant customer history.
- A project management tool that creates tasks, tracks progress, and identifies risks automatically.
- An HR platform that streamlines onboarding, policies, and employee requests.
- A procurement system that automates approvals and vendor workflows.
- A customer portal that unifies data across multiple systems and personalizes every interaction.
- An operations dashboard that helps teams monitor systems and act on issues in real time.
- A knowledge management platform that helps employees find answers instantly.

The only requirement

Your project must use Xano as the backend in a meaningful way.

Show us how you're using Xano to power your application's business logic, APIs, workflows, authentication, integrations, or data model.

Everything else is up to you.

## What to Submit

- Project name + one-line pitch

- 2–4 minute demo showing the application end-to-end
- Build story

  - Why did you choose it?
  - Which AI tools did you use? (Claude Code, Cursor, etc.)
  - Approximately how long did it take to build?
  - What would have taken significantly longer without AI + Xano?
