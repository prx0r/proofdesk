## Key Rules

- Teams should build apps from scratch (Mobile App / Consumer Web App / Business Web App)
- Teams can solve as many "Challenges" as desired (zero, one, or multiple)
- Teams must be between 1 - 5 members
- Teams with over 5 members are not eligible to win anything
- All "Challenges" are hosted by sponsors. The sponsors will choose their own prize winners
- To be considered for the Overall Winner — teams must submit by Thursday, September 3 at 10:00 AM PST

---

## Overall Judging Criteria

**Round 1 (10:00 AM - 12:00 PM PT):** Overall Judges review projects and fill out score cards

**Round 2 (10:00 AM - 12:00 PM PT):** Sponsor Judges review projects and fill out score cards

Overall Judges evaluate based on:

1. **Progress** — How much progress did you make?
2. **Concept** — Does it solve a real problem?
3. **Feasibility** — Could this become a startup or company?

Top 5 project teams pitch on Main Stage. Winners announced at 3:30 PM.

---

## Submission Requirements

Required at time of project creation:
- What's Your Project Called
- Here's the Elevator Pitch
- Created By (team members)

Recommended before submission deadline:
- It's Built With (technologies used)
- Image Gallery (screenshots)
- Try It Out (link/URL to project)
- Video Demo

Then select which Sponsor Challenges / Prizes you're working on (can be zero or multiple).
